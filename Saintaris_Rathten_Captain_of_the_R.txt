Saintaris Rathten, Captain of the Royal Guard, Fall of a nation

---

3rd of May, 1009

Seven hours till Luna event

I awake as I usually do, bolt upright, breathing heavily and covered in sweat. My one eye focuses quickly in the darkness of the room, a calm washing over me as I recognise the familiar surroundings of my own quarters. It was just a dream, a bad memory that I am forever going to live with, the moment I lost my eye. I take a moment to let my heart stop beating so hard, falling back into the plush silken sheets, letting my mind recognise the new reality that it actually dwells in.

My sleep is often plagued with my own memories, the things I have seen on the battlefield. I've seen friends die, killed men, boys even, in defense of the realm. I push down my feelings and move to get out of bed. The pain and guilt which my night terrors stir up quickly fade as I compose myself, I would do it all again if my duty asked it of me. To protect my kingdom, Alastia, to protect it's citizens, every woman and girl in this land. To protect them from the nation of men that border us on all sides, the men who never cease to have our nation for their own. Soon the only discomfort I feel is the dull throbbing pain where my right eye sits. Milky white and useless, intersected by a deep scar, my black hair quickly falls down to hide it as I reach to my side table to put on my eyepatch. Pressing it against my unworking eye, it clamps onto my face with a dull light, the magic which blesses the one of a kind artefact holding it tight to my face without strapping or other obvious physical means. The pain leaves me and feels comfortable, almost normal. As much as I miss my eye, I cannot help myself in the pride I feel when wearing my patch. The Great Mage of our kingdom, Serizenza, forged it personally and I wear it with more pride than any medal I possess, of which I have my fair share. The dull haze of dawn seemed to be approaching over the mountains as it fills my room through the window. It is time to collect myself and attack the day. As per usual, my time is precious and I am sure to remain busy at every turn, the early hours are the only time I have to myself.

My usual routine remains the same as it is everyday, dressing down in just basic cloth I start using the facilities available to me in the barracks before many of my troops have yet woken up. I spend the same amount of time each day, honing my skill with my blade, hacking precise chunks out of the wooden training dummies. Moving the Atlas stones from pillar to pillar to keep my strength up. To finish my routine I always like to end it by jogging all the way along the curtain wall of the main castle to make sure I have the stamina for combat.

I always sprint the last steps of the highest turret, making sure I always push my body to its maximum. At least that's what I tell myself, to be honest, I just prefer to catch my breath at my favourite place in the entire city. High enough to see most of the city beneath me and far out into the distance beyond the city walls. Leaning forward against the stone, I suck in the air my lungs crave as I look out and take in the view of my Kingdom, my home, Alastia. Battlements and fortifications stretched a short way out from my position, the barracks were only a small part of the central palace. It's high impressive white towers stretched high above the city. I could see the early morning traders even from this distance, the streets were still quiet but I knew that would soon change, who knew what kind of problems would find it's way onto my desk once the city truly wakes up.

My gaze moves from the streets of our capital and out into the wilderness beyond, my country is arid and not particularly temperate. Unlike the forests of the northern Kingdoms, vast plains stretch forth all the way to the mountains in the west. The sight always fills me with awe. Not so much for the distance which can be observed but the sight of one of those mountains is always so unbelievable that people would think you are lying if you told them about it. Splitpeak sits many miles away from the rest of the sierra, it is called so because of it's obvious double peak, it looked more like two giant piles of rock next to each other. Even as a little girl, the story always filled me with awe, the entire mountain was split in the middle as it impacted with the ground. The whole thing was just picked up and moved from the rest of the range, many, many leagues and dropped down from a great height, splitting the mountain peak asunder. If the stories that I heard as a child were true, under that mountain lies a vast 'unbeatable army' with terrifying giant beasts and engines of war. All this happened over two hundred years ago, it saved the kingdom from oblivion so they say. The same thought always enters my mind whenever I see that mountain. The force that caused this cataclysm to happen was the same force that held my patch in place. This was the power of Serizenza, truly she is a mighty sorceress. Two hundred years ago she stepped into the conflict and dropped a mountain on an incoming army. I have no idea how old Serizenza is, she has been at the side of the royal family for as long as anyone can remember. Watching successive Queens come and go, always at their beck and call, advising them as necessary. I am glad she is on our side, but I do wonder why I risk my life fighting defensive wars against incoming armies of men when she can just obliterate them with a wave of her hand.

I cast away the thought, it was not my place to question Serizenza or the Queen. Should they want to risk my life, for what ever reason, they have but to ask. I will full fill my duty. I take one last look at the broken mountain and finish my exercise.

Five hours till Luna Event

Returning to my quarters after such strenuous exercise always seems to leave me with more energy than when I started, I washed quickly to remove the sweat from my body and started one of my most important rituals of the day. Donning my plate armour. It was heavy and intricate, fine blue silken cloth hung from one side as well as many small glittering jewels which were imbedded into the right breast, each one symbolising a battle I had been in or some other important distinction I had earned. The crest of our kingdom flourished my cape's surface. I made sure the material was not creased in anyway and looked as vibrant and pristine as the day it was made.

I checked myself in the full body mirror before I left, my long black hair was plaited neatly behind me, every piece of my armour was in place, my sword hung at my side. Everything looked in order. As Captain of all the military in the Royal palace, it was important I set the best example. We were no run of the mill town guard, we were the elite and as the top of the pyramid, I have to be the best of the best. My one eye locked onto its mirrored reflection as I took a deep breath and turned away, I gave a small prayer to our Goddess and headed out the door.

The halls were now busy, armoured guards in pairs of two patrolled the main walls, every one I past on my way to my main office gives me a crisp salute, their right hand reaching across themselves to place it on their opposite shoulder. As I out ranked them I didn't feel the need to return the salutes, my mind already mulling over some of the mundane tasks I had put off from yesterday to deal with today.

I walked into my office without stopping to salute the guard outside, the thick oak wood of the door giving way as I pushed it open. My desk loomed in front of me, I tried to keep it neat but alas, this city always has a problem that begs my attention and it usually does so by magically transforming itself into paperwork and flinging itself all over my desk in messy piles. One thing was different however, a black feather sat directly in the middle of the mess on top of everything, making it hard to miss. I knew instantly what that meant.

I moved to pull the curtain across the one small window which looked out over the city and then walked over and bolted the door. The feather was a calling card of the Order of the Vigilant, a very secretive organisation which acted as the eyes and ears of Alastia. They were our military intelligence, spy network and secret police all in one. If they wanted to speak to me, it must be more important than all the paper on my desk put together. As much as I dreaded facing the mundane drudgery of my job, I feared that the Order of the Vigilant had an even worse problem for me, they always did.

As soon as I secure the room a woman appears right in front of me, her invisibility cloaking spell could be turned on and off at will, most agents of the Order had them permanently on, they even like to sleep invisible. It was very rare to actually see one, however the woman in front of me in her light black cloth ninja-yoroi was familiar, despite her preference to not be seen. She was Alki Vermory, the de-facto head of the Order, I knew her well, many times have we relied on each other to further the safety of the kingdom. I respected her, she was good at her job, I hope she feels the same about myself.

"Saintaris" She gave me a salute as she greeted me.

I was fairly sure I out rank her in the grand scheme of things, different Orders and command structures can get complicated in Alastia but I return the gesture all the same, professional courtesy if nothing else.

"Alki" I said giving her a small nod. "This is unexpected, I hope your presence here is just a friendly visit, no great catastrophe is about to happen I hope."

"Well no, I do not think so, I am here to make you aware of a developing situation that the Order has been tracking, its something which I thought you might need to know."

"Please continue..." I gestured to the seat across from my desk as I took the time to sit down, give my body a little rest from the heavy armor which clung to my limbs. She remained standing as she always did, I didn't expect her to stay long after she told me everything she needed to.

"Early yesterday morning, one of our agents monitored a male cross the southern border. He was on foot but had made good speed to this city, he should be here within the hour. " Alki stated.

I mulled over the words a moment.

"The southern border? He came from Hastaluna?" I asked.

"Yes it looks likely" Alki confirmed.

"That explains why you didn't intercept him, Hastaluna is the only country that hasn't tried to invade us. I thought they would be happy keeping to themselves..." I muttered to myself finding it annoying that now even this small peaceful country was making a problem of itself.

"That was the opinion of the Order as well, Hastaluna is very small and secretive country, we have never seen anyone cross into our lands from there... We are keeping him under observation for now, see what we can learn from surveying him. Once he enters your city however, its up to you how you want to play this."

"Hmmm, and you haven't noticed anything out of the ordinary?" I asked, Alki shook her head. "Well, our laws are clear, no man can enter unless invited. I understand you wish to find out what he wants, but I must uphold the law."

"I understand and it's why I brought it to you. It's your call. Should you bring him in, it also might give as a fresh opportunity to get answers." Alki tapped a finger on her lip as she thought out loud.

"You say he is coming from the south? That would put him in the market district, if he could even get past the gate guards." I asked.

"He has disguised himself, covered head to toe, your guards are there to stop trouble not search everyone in and out, unless he lets his disguise slip, he is probably in the market as we speak. "

"Well then..." I exclaim as I rise to my feet "Lets find out what he wants." I move to the door and unbolt it, before I open it I indulge myself a little sentimentality "It's nice to work with you ag-" I turn to Alki but am met with nothing but an empty room. She always was all business and I am not surprised she had already re cloaked.

Three hours and forty minutes till Luna Event

A short journey out of the palace and past the Temple to the Goddess bought me to the market district, I take a look around to get my bearings. I find my mark instantly, the disguise did little to hide his bulking male features. He was clearly the tallest around, wrapped in black cloth as he studied a fruit stall in front of him. So far nothing to out of the ordinary behaviour wise other than he looked like he was trying to be inconspicuous as little too much. I pushed my back up against the wall as I spied on him from afar, mulling over in my head whether to put him under arrest or stake him out some more, perhaps he was here to meet someone, although I dismissed the idea out of hand. What business would any woman have with a man?

I narrowed my vision on the events as they played out, my choice to arrest him or not was to be made for me it would seem, as even with my one eye I could see commotion out of my periphery. His disguise had not held up which shouldn't be surprising. One of the women who works the stall was complaining to the guards, pointing over to the oblivious cloaked man. I watched as the lead guard adjusted her armour and made her way over to him. I recognised her as the district leader, she was brash, more interested in the authority the position gave her than fulfilling her duty to her charges. I had denied her a promotion myself, twice. A decision which I could see was the right one, she was being sloppy. She was approaching him straight up, with only one other guard. No one was covering the most obvious escape route he would take if he bolted. I moved from my vantage point and took up position just round the corner from him as I watched the guard put a hand on his shoulder.

"Hey! Hey! Hey!" She shouted as she spun him around. "I've had word that a suspicious person is in town, that wouldn't be you would it?"

I couldn't really see much from my vantage point but I could sense that she didn't have a defense up and when he turned away from her she left herself completely open.

"Don't turn your back on me- AAH" The man had grabbed some of the spice dishes from the stall next to him, his hand flipping the whole thing over, catching her completely by surprise as the colourful cloud enveloped her. Then he lifted his leg and kicked out, catching her square in the breastplate, sending her falling backwards. In her blinded flailing, she grabbed onto her other guard, dragging her down to the floor with her. In a split-second the man used the opportunity he created to sprint away, right down the escape path that I predicted he would, I braced myself for what I knew would be coming.

He rounded the corner in a panic, fabric still covering much of his face, obscuring his view as he shoved a poor girl to the floor who shrieked in surprise. One more market seller gestured at him as he barged past her, pushing her to the side. Then in his haste, he ran straight into me. The impact made me skid backwards half a foot but I had braced myself, even though he was an adult male, even larger than me, I was in full battle plate. He staggered backwards a step, surprised his momentum has suddenly stopped. I watched his movement carefully with one hand on my sword. As if in slow motion I saw him ball his fist and adjust his footing, I knew a right haymaker was coming. I leaned back in preparation and watched his fist sail by my face, hitting nothing. I shot out my own gauntleted fist and grasped him at the wrist, tugging in the same direction he punched, using his own momentum to carry him off balance. My left hand held my sword hilt tightly as I unsheathed it, sliding the honour blade out of its scabbard halfway, but incredibly quickly. It's metal jewelled hilt slammed into him, just below the ribs, causing him to groan in pain. My right hand, still grasping his, then pulled his arm back over himself spinning him around. My heavy iron clad boot swept across and took out his legs, sending him to the dusty floor, which he fell onto in a mangled heap. To make sure he doesn't get any other ideas I took a step froward and put all my weight on his back, pinning him there as he squirmed in pain.

"Out of my way! Get out the way!" The guard shouted rounding the corner. Her eyes first saw the man on the floor then followed up my leg to see me standing on top of him. Her angry scowl quickly turned into one of surprised panic.

"C-Captain! What? I-I..." She was at a lost for words, so simply gave up speaking and resigned to give me a salute and stood at attention. Her face contorted in worry and twitching from the spices in her eyes, which she did her best to ignore. More guards spilled in around me, spears pointing down at the man under my foot.

"Sloppy work..." I muttered making sure the guard heard me. "What was your name again?"

"Geris D-District leader, Geris Mam!" She croaked unable to maintain eye contact with me.

"Take him away" I ordered the surrounding guards to apprehend the gasping man on the floor as I stepped off him. "Next time, watch your escape routes and do not underestimate a man. They can be dangerous." I glared at her, taking her down a peg.

"Y-yes Mam!" Is all Geris said in response.

I could tell she wasn't taking anything I said onboard. She probably wanted nothing more than for me to leave and make her the biggest fish in the pond again. I don't have time to worry about petty rank politics right now, I would be wasting my breath dressing her down any more so I simply dismissed her and turned my back to return to the holding cells. I pretended not to pay any mind to the crowd that had gathered and watched the whole thing happen. Everyone knows who I am, I could hear the gossip and chatter spark up as I left the market. I kept the same stoic expression on my face, unflinching, cold. I want everyone to know that their Captain of the Guard can handle trouble and it didn't even phase me. Deep down, I know that looked good, I secretly enjoyed the admiration. I checked my thoughts quickly however, for now I need to focus on the man problem I have on my hands.

Fifty seven minutes till Luna Event

I stood outside the cell door, my gauntleted fist resting on the handle before I entered, collecting my thoughts. I had a brief written report from the field agent who was monitoring him ever since he crossed the border. It was light on details, but I didn't doubt the agent's competence. If there were few details, there wasn't much to report on. That itself raised more questions. I don't like it, I don't like that there was now a man in our city and I don't like that I have no idea what he is doing here. I pushed the door open and entered, quickly taking stock of the room. He sat in irons on the floor at one end of the room, chains making sure his movement was very much restricted. I could see he was in what looked like loose rags and nothing else. The sack like material, making ruddy pants and a tunic that was mostly open at the front showing most of his chest. He had the look of a vagrant, not a trained assassin or foreign agent. A slight sneer pasted my lips involuntarily, if he were female it would almost be indecent with how much of his skin was showing. I noted the sparse hair covering his chest were bosoms should be, Men truly were ugly creatures. A metal collar with a small jewel at it's center had been fixed around his neck which wasn't on him when he was captured, giving him the look of a caged animal. Two guards on the other end of the room were keeping a close eye on him, their halberds close at hand in case he tried anything, although I am sure they have faith in me to handle myself if he does try violence.

"Soo..." I began speaking up and getting the man's attention. "It is unusual we get guests from Hastaluna. I don't suppose you want to tell us exactly why you crossed our borders and came to our city?"

The man looked at me dead in the eye, this by itself was unusual. I have had my fair share of interrogations and nearly all of them were timid, avoided eye contact. He still had confidence in him, like he was still in control of the situation. I assume this was mostly just male arrogance at work, although it might be a thread to pull at later.

"I was just going for a walk..." He half joked. The jewel on the collar glowed red then a dull orange, getting the man's attention. "What the?"

"That is a truth seeker. It will tell me if you are lying with the color it glows." I explained to the now shocked man. "It's early and I have already had a very hard day, so I would appreciate if you would be a little bit more honest from this point out?" My words were polite but I made no attempt to hide the threat under my words. He gulped hard and looked a little more worried about his situation before giving me a small nod. It's amazing how much easier it is to threaten people when you have already practically curb stomped them in the same day.

"Good" I said cheerily. "So, what is your name?"

"Jara Lunta" The man told me, the jewel glowed a pale blue, very much the truth.

"Well Jara, what are you doing in my city?" I asked.

The man started to sweat, looking around a little desperate before landing his eyes on me.

"I-I've come a long way, could I at least get something to sit on? My legs are so tired." He asked.

So stalling was his game, I noted. No problem, he can't stall forever, I will get the truth out of him sooner or later.

"Fetch a chair!" I barked out to one of the guards who gave a crisp nod before leaving the room.

"Thank you" The man smiled a weary smile.

"You can thank me by telling me why you are here"

"It's a long story..." He started, his hand wearily rubbing his collar.

"We are not going anywhere I assure you." I told him as the guard returned with a small wooden stool.

The man grinned at the sight, eager to finally get to sit down.

"Than-" He started to thank me before I took the stool from the guard and sat on it myself right in front of him.

"Let's hear this long story and try to keep things honest. " I told him, looking up from my seated position.

A bitter look played across the man's face, realising the predicament he is in.

"My country.... Hastaluna...." He started trying to forced the words out. I just looked at him with wrapped attention, my eye glued to his Truth Seeker. "It is going to attack Alastia"

The jewel glowed blue, he was telling the truth. I wasn't shocked so much by the revelation. It was the only nation that shares a boarder that hasn't invaded our country, men are violent monsters who will stop at nothing to claim what they want. It was only a matter of time. What did surprise me is why was he here, why warn us?

"I see..." I made no outward emotion to his claim, best to keep him guessing. "and why are you here? Are you part of the attack?"

The man looked incredibly nervous, sweat dripping from his brow.

"It's hot in here..." He mutter to himself. The truth seeker around his neck glowed a dull blue, he wasn't lying about that, the heat of the Alastian day could even reach to the holding cells. His eyes flicked down to the jewel as it's light faded away, then back to me. I could tell, something was going on behind those eyes, he was thinking of something.

"I- I am here to betray my country..." He said plainly. The jewel on his neck glowed blue again. As far as I was aware, the Truth Seeker was imprecise but impossible to fool. His intention was against that of his country.

I sat there for a second, actually surprised. Treachery was never a mind set I could understand, duty and loyalty were my life. I would face death before betraying my oaths, a hundred times over. Yet here was a man, willing to throw it all away, and on the face of it, to the benefit of my country. Was I really that fortunate?

"You wish to betray your country?" I asked raising my eye brows. "Then why try to run? Why not just hand yourself in?"

Jara took a moment to think, looking me directly in my eye. "I panicked a little bit..." The jewel started to glow a slight warm yellow making him re think his statement.

"I-I mean the time. It wasn't right..." He stammered correcting himself, the jewel turned a deep blue. It was the truest statement he had said all day.

"What do you mean the time wasn't right? The more time we have the better." I started to argue but checked myself. He was being deliberately evasive, the Truth Seeker was making him pick his words very carefully. He might be betraying his country but I still do not trust him.

"It's complicated" He said, the jewel agreed with it's color. " Hastaluna is small but it's power resides in the heavenly bodies, you know this, right?"

I tend not to get involved with the details of other nations. Such matters are what the Order of the Vigilant is for, but from what I did know about our small southern neighbour, is it consisted of small dense townships at high altitude. Many of their biggest religious structures also functioned as observatories to study the heavens. They were, what I thought until this morning, harmless moon worshippers, even it's name mentioned the moon.

"Hastaluna has no power." I corrected him. "If it has armies, I've never heard of them."

"We don't need armies!" He spat back at me defiantly. His jewel glowed orange.

"I didn't need the Truth seeker to see through that one" I quipped at him. The man looked down at his betraying jewel, with a little bit of sadness behind his eyes.

"We have our strengths, the crystal moon is all we really need." He muttered, the jewel turning a pale white marking his words closer to the truth.

"There is no 'we' any more, Mr Lunta or have you forgotten your intent to sell out your country?" I reminded him, before he could retort the cell door opened and one of the Royal guards entered, complete in her finery. Unusual to see one this far from the throne room, I looked at her quizzically. She gave me a quick salute, took a quick look at the prisoner and leaned in close to speak softly in my ear so the prisoner couldn't hear.

"The Queen has summoned you to the main throne room, she and Serizenza wishes to see the prisoner for themselves." The guard efficiently told me in a hushed tone.

"When?!" I asked back confused, my one eye leaving the man and finding the guard's face, conveying my seriousness.

"Immediately" The guard told me before straitening up and giving me a nod and a salute. She turned and left the room without another word, eager to get back to her duties now her errand had be run. I stared at the closed door in her wake, slightly stunned at how control of the situation had been taken out of my hands like someone tugging a carpet out from under my feet. I turned my gaze to glare at the bedraggled man in front of me, mulling over what to do next.

"Is there a problem?" Jara asked, not having access to our exchange.

"Perhaps..." I said to him cryptically, rising to my feet. "You have been honest enough for now..." I walked towards him, wooden stool in hand. ".. I shall return in a moment, better get some rest while you can." I placed the stool in front of him and motioned to sit as I turned and exited the cell. He beamed a broad smile and took his seat with a relieved exhale.

Outside in the hallway I turned to the guard.

"I want Estragar and Gallama to report to this cell, immediately!" I barked the order with such force the guard almost jumped out of her armour. Perhaps my frustration with the situation was showing through too much. Estragar and Gallama were two of my best soldiers, I didn't like how this was playing out and in my gut I feared trouble. I didn't like that the man was still an unknown entity, and he was going to be in the presence of the Queen. It was too risky in my book, but I cannot deny my highnesses' request.

I looked left and right in the empty, quiet hallway. It seemed like no one was around, but I had a hunch that I wasn't alone.

"I don't like it. Have you found anything on your end?" I asked thin air. I suspected Alki was near, if she wasn't, no one saw me make a fool of myself. If she was...

Alki Appeared in front of me with a surprised, impressed look in her eyes. I gave her a decent poker face and simply tilted my head, raising my eye brows.

"Yes, actually. Something interesting..." Alki told me while fetching a small leather pouch from her belt. "The guards confiscated what little he had on him. This..." Alki Held up the pouch and motioned that she was going to pour it out. "... is VERY interesting. Do not let it touch your skin."

I held out my gauntleted hand and let a small pearl like stone drop from the pouch onto the hard leather covered palm. It was no bigger than a pea.

"What is it?" I asked studding the plain looking object.

"It's a Controller jewel. It's currently not active but when triggered it can bend the will of whoever touches it to the person who activated the object." Alki told me plainly, always right to the point.

"I've never seen anything like this" I admitted.

"Nor should you" Alki told me. "It's highly secret, purely need to know only. Those orders come from Serizenza herself. I am only telling you now because of the importance of what he just told you in there and to let you know that he isn't telling you the whole truth."

I nodded, understanding my place in the overall chain of command. I banished any sourness on being left out of the loop from my mind, I need to focus on the problem at hand when the security of our nation is at stake.

"How rare are these things? Could there be others?" I asked.

"Very rare and no. I've only come across these things once before in all my years in the Order. Three years ago there was a plot to infiltrate Alastia with three girls who had come to us as refugees. The controllers were surgically implanted under the skin, impossible to see. All three of the jewels were sourced from several nations in a conspiracy against us. We believe that it took the great wealth and resources of the combined nations just to acquire three of these things. We also suspect they were creating sleeper cells, planted to be used against us at a later date. "

"How did you foil it?" I asked, wondering how the Order always seems to be in the right place at the right time.

"In this case?" Alki raised an eyebrow. Serizenza. She can sense them, even from great distance, she is also the only force capable of destroying these things. We are under orders to bring all Controllers to her."

"We should get this to her right away then, she needs to know." I told her.

"If it's in this city, she already knows. I suspect it is no coincidence that he was summoned to court. When you get a chance, you should get the jewel to her as quickly as possible. I will continue my investigation as you meet the Queen."

"What do you think was Hastaluna's plan with the Controller?" I asked mulling over the question. "To control Serizenza herself?"

"Unlikely" Alki shook her head. "If it is that, they are fools, Serizenza can destroy the Controllers, she is almost certainly defended against them. I doubt a Controller could even effect her. I would be most concerned with the Queen or Princesses, although I cannot see how he hoped to get near them. He is most likely to have been tasked with infiltrating the nobility in some way." She mused.

"For what point and purpose?" I asked looking at the small shiny thing in my palm.

"We have been monitoring troop movements in the Eastern nations. They do appear to be massing near Hastaluna's border. An invasion is likely, perhaps Hastaluna seeks influence, an attempt to drag us into the petty wars of men. As it stands, the aggressors seem to be keeping well clear of our border."

The aggressions of our neighbours was news to me. Had the threat been pointed at us I am sure the Order would have informed me sooner, I trusted their assessment that a conflict to our south need not concern us.

"I best prepare the prisoner to meet the Queen, I cannot let them wait any longer." I informed Alki. "I don't like it though, I feel like we are still missing something."

"I share your suspicions." Alki told me.

"Have two of your agents in the main hall as extra security. I want to be prepared for anything."

"I shall make it so. I will investigate further while you meet the Queen."

I nodded to her as she quickly vanished right in front of me, just as the sound of armoured boots came from down the hall. I pocketed the jewel in a pouch on my belt before turning to the soldiers coming down the passageway. Estragar and Gallama marched in precise lockstep behind the guard who was sent to fetch them. The guard took up her post on the door as the two more finely dressed soldiers presented a well drilled salute at me.

"As requested Captain!" Estragar shouted.

"Listen up!" I barked. "We are going to escort the prisoner to the throne room, and it's going to go by the book. We don't know what he is up to, so keep alert at all times. "

"Yes Sir!" They both replied. They were without a doubt the best soldiers I have had the honour of commanding. I felt a little more in control now I had them under my direct command.

I turned and re-entered the cell, seeing the prisoner right where I left him, sitting on the rickety stool, hands bound in chains.
